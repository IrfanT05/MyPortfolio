A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm Irfan.
I make tech feel simpler.

BSc Computer Science · First Class Honours Manchester Metropolitan University

I'm a Manchester-based Computer Science graduate and IT professional. I've worked in frontline IT support at Manchester Metropolitan University, helping students and staff with everyday technology in a large, busy education environment.

From PCs, loan laptops and network printers to software, accounts and AV checks, that work involved investigating problems, documenting support tickets and knowing when to escalate. My Digital and Peer Guide work also helped me explain unfamiliar technology clearly.

That experience taught me that most people want someone who listens, understands the problem and explains what happens next. I bring the same approach to small businesses that need help with their websites and everyday IT.

My technical background

HTML, CSS and JavaScript for the web; Python, Java and C# for software and data tasks; SQL/MySQL, REST APIs, Flutter/Dart and Firebase. I also use Git, GitHub and Postman to manage and test my work.
